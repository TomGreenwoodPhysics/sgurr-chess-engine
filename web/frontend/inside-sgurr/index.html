Inside Sgurr

Two moving parts.

Every move Sgurr plays comes from two systems working together. One decides which moves are worth looking at. The other decides how good the resulting positions are. Neither is any use on its own, and each has its own lab below.

01 · Search

Which moves to look at

Sgurr cannot examine every move — the tree is far too large. It looks a few moves ahead, then deeper, then deeper again, cutting away branches that cannot beat what it has already found. The move it prefers can change as it goes.

02 · Evaluation

How good a position is

At the end of every line the search needs a number. Sgurr gets it from a small neural network that reads the position — which piece sits on which square — and returns a score in pawns.

03 · Together

One move

The search asks the network about millions of positions per second, and the answers steer where it looks next. A better network makes the search sharper; a deeper search makes the network's judgement count for more.
